# 📊 SMAPIS Quant

## AI-Assisted Market Intelligence & Decision-Support Platform

**SMAPIS Quant** is an AI-assisted market-intelligence platform developed by **SMAPIS Technologies — Smart Multi-Agent Platform & Intelligent Systems**.

The platform is designed to help users discover, evaluate, simulate and monitor market opportunities using a combination of structured analytics, risk controls, historical replay, portfolio intelligence and AI-supported decision systems.

SMAPIS Quant is being developed as a research and intelligence platform rather than a simple signal generator.

Its objective is to combine:

**Data → Analysis → Risk Evaluation → AI Assessment → Simulation → Portfolio Intelligence**

---

# 🎯 The Problem

Market participants often face several challenges:

* Too much fragmented market data
* Difficulty comparing opportunities consistently
* Inconsistent risk evaluation
* Emotional decision-making
* Limited visibility into historical performance
* Poor portfolio-level intelligence
* Lack of explainable decision support

Many trading tools focus only on signals.

SMAPIS Quant is designed to go further by bringing opportunity discovery, risk analysis, historical context, simulation and AI-assisted assessment into one platform.

---

SMAPIS Quant provides a structured market-intelligence environment where opportunities can be:

* Discovered

# ⏪ Opportunity Replay

The Opportunity Replay module allows historical opportunities to be reconstructed and reviewed.

This can help users understand:

* What the market looked like at the time
* What information was available
* What decision the system would have made
* How the opportunity developed
* How risk changed over time
* Whether the original thesis remained valid

Replay is designed to improve explainability and post-decision learning.
